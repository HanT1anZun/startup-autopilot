# Opportunity research and scoring

Generate ten genuinely distinct, current directions from the user's profile and live evidence. Do not start from a fixed catalog and do not promise uniqueness.

## Research contract

- Name the buyer, painful job, bounded deliverable, likely price logic, reachable channel, smallest test, and principal risk for every direction.
- Use current public sources and record the URL or local evidence reference plus observation date.
- Prefer primary sources for prices and capabilities, direct buyer language for pain, and independent sources for market context.
- Keep external inspiration attributed. Competitor existence shows a category; it does not prove this user can win.
- Separate facts from interpretation with `confirmed`, `attributed`, `inferred`, and `unknown` labels.
- Do not use sensitive profile details in public searches.

## Score

Score every axis from 0 to 100 and state the reason:

- Demand and willingness-to-pay evidence: 25%
- Reachable buyers: 20%
- User asset fit: 20%
- Speed to a reversible validation: 15%
- Plausible unit economics: 10%
- Delivery, legal, and operational risk control: 10%

A missing demand source caps `demand_evidence` at 20. No reachable channel caps `reachable_buyers` at 20. A regulated, prohibited, deceptive, or rights-infringing model is rejected rather than down-scored.

## Output

Submit exactly ten objects to `rank`. Present the controller's ranking with evidence gaps and the fastest disconfirming test. Recommend one, but let the user select unless they explicitly delegated selection inside the current task.
