# Discovery

Ask one adaptive question at a time. Briefly connect it to what the user already shared without repeating private answers verbatim. Accept `prefer_not_to_say` when the answer is not essential; never pressure the user to reveal employer, customer, credential, financial, health, or identity data.

## Ten fields

1. `outcome`: What concrete first-revenue outcome matters, and why now?
2. `success_proof`: What small observable result would prove the effort is worth continuing?
3. `constraints`: What time, cash, geography, language, and risk constraints apply during the next month?
4. `assets`: What proven skills, access, audience, tools, reputation, or reusable assets can be used legitimately?
5. `past_attempts`: What has already been tried, what happened, and where did it fail?
6. `boundaries`: What legal, ethical, employer, privacy, identity, or lifestyle limits must be respected?
7. `reachable_buyers`: Which real buyer groups can be reached without private employer or customer data?
8. `work_preferences`: What recurring work is energizing, tolerable, or unacceptable?
9. `market_tolerance`: What rejection, silence, interviews, or public testing is the user willing to face?
10. `execution_system`: What cadence, deadline, and accountability mechanism will keep one campaign moving?

## Quality gate

Discovery is complete when all ten fields are answered or explicitly skipped. Do not advance to opportunity research when assets, boundaries, and reachable buyers are all unknown; return the smallest safe information-gathering step instead.

Answers are working hypotheses, not objective facts. Never transmit raw answers as search terms or to third parties. Convert only the minimum non-sensitive attributes needed for research into generic queries.
